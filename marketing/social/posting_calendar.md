## Boost plan

1. **Main boosts:** c1 (launch), c2 (ProveIt), c4 (meal scan), c6 (referral closer and retargeting).
2. **Secondary boosts:** c3 Mansi. Keep the creative factual: AI coach, not a doctor.
3. **Conditional boosts:** p04 and p08 memes, only if their organic share rate beats the account average. Memes that need a push don't land.
4. **Targeting:** Android users in India. Run separate English and Hinglish creative sets and compare. Start in cities with live partner venues, and don't promise venues in cities where none are live yet.
5. **Objective:** start with reach/engagement on days 1–3, then switch to app installs to the Play Store link once the pixel/SDK has data.
6. **Stop rule:** pause any boost whose cost per install is about 2× the campaign median after 48 hours, and move the budget to the best performer.

## Always-on rules for the two weeks

- **Reply to comments within 2 hours** in the 8 AM–11 PM window, in the language the commenter used (Hinglish if they wrote Hinglish).
- **No fake social proof.** Don't post "1,000 downloads!" style milestones, and don't repost user content without written permission. Real member reposts (with consent, faces blurred where they ask) can start in week 2.
- **Highlights to set up on day 1:** Pass · ProveIt · Mansi · Meal Scan · Circles · Refer.
- **Link in bio:** the Play Store URL with UTM parameters, e.g. `&utm_source=instagram&utm_campaign=launch` (and `utm_source=linkedin` / `x` on the other channels). The in-app referral link already adds `&referrer=<code>`.
- **iOS questions:** reply "Android first, on Google Play. We'll shout when there's more." Don't promise a date.
- **Weekly review (Sun night):** saves, shares, profile visits and Play Store clicks per asset. Double down on the top 2 themes in the next sprint.
